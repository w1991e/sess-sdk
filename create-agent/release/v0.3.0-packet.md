## What 0.3.0 contains

The `0.3.0` entry in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md). Compared with `0.2.0`:

- The template depends exactly on `@sess.si/sdk` `0.5.0` (published on `latest`, SHA-256
  `84f6e005a849158d149100358c75d576b0725178f734aa0d7f4ced22e79c7f24`, the pin in
  [`release/sdk-candidate.json`](../../release/sdk-candidate.json)); 0.2.0 pinned `0.4.0`.
- Sess's tighter text limits in the template: each plan option is "idea, part of day", such as
  `Picnic in the park, afternoon`, at most 30 characters; the ideas in `template/weather.mjs` are
  shorter, and the template README's numbers follow (#77).
- User-Agent `sess-create-agent/0.3.0`.
- Nothing else in the packed files (`index.mjs`, `template/`, `README.md`, `LICENSE`,
  `package.json`) changed since `create-agent-v0.2.0`:
  `git diff create-agent-v0.2.0 -- create-agent/index.mjs create-agent/template create-agent/README.md create-agent/LICENSE create-agent/package.json`
  shows only `package.json`'s version and `template/README.md`, `template/agent.mjs`,
  `template/package.json` and `template/weather.mjs`.
